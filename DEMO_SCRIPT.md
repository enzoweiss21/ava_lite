# Ava Mirror - Demo Script

> **Target:** 60-90 seconds for maximum impact

## Pre-Demo Checklist

- [ ] `npm run dev` is running
- [ ] Browser tab open to `localhost:3000`
- [ ] Zoom level at 100% for best visibility
- [ ] Browser console closed (unless debugging)
- [ ] Prepare to speak confidently!

---

## Script (60-90 seconds)

### Opening (10 seconds)
**[Screen shows Ava's avatar and greeting]**

> "I wanted to reimagine what an 'AI employee' looks like. This is **Ava Mirror** — an interface where Ava explains her decisions in real-time, like a transparent teammate."

---

### Demo Flow (50-60 seconds)

#### 1. Show Live Task Feed (10 sec)
**[Point to the task feed updating]**

> "Ava's working autonomously on lead scoring, email drafting, and sequence management. Every 3 seconds, a new task rotates in."

**[Wait for a task to appear]**

> "Here she paused Sequence A. Let's ask why..."

---

#### 2. Click "Why?" Button (15 sec)
**[Click the "Why?" button]**

> "Ava explains: 'I paused the sequence because bounce rate hit 9% over 1,000 sends, and seed inboxes flagged deliverability risk.'"

**[Scroll through the reasoning panel]**

> "She shows confidence level — 83% — the signals she used, and alternatives she considered. Transparency without black boxes."

---

#### 3. Ask a Follow-up Question (15 sec)
**[Type in follow-up question: "What would you do if reply volume drops?"]**

**[Click "Ask"]**

> "Now I can ask follow-ups... 'What would you do if reply volume drops?'"

**[Ava responds]**

> "She answers contextually: 'I'd check if subject lines are engaging, test new personalization variables, and analyze which segments are responding...'"

---

#### 4. Show Another Task (10 sec)
**[Close panel, wait for new task]**

> "Let's check another decision — Lead Scoring for Acme Robotics."

**[Click "Why?" on the new task]**

> "'Score = 0.72 because of recent funding, HubSpot stack, and hiring SDRs.' All the evidence right there."

---

### Closing (10 seconds)

> "This is all running on Next.js with a lightweight LLM integration. Give me 2 weeks and I'll wire this into your live sequence telemetry and deliverability feeds."

**[Pause, smile]**

> "Ava can be your transparent AI teammate. Want to see more?"

---

## Questions You Might Get

### "How does it know what to explain?"
> "Right now it's prompting GPT-4 with the decision context — task, signals, confidence, alternatives. In production, this would pull from your actual telemetry: bounce rates, reply classifiers, sequence state."

### "Can users override Ava's decisions?"
> "Absolutely. You could add an approval mode where Ava explains first, waits for a thumbs up, then acts. Or log overrides to improve her model over time."

### "What happens if there's no internet / API is down?"
> "It falls back to deterministic mock responses. The interface still works, you just don't get dynamic explanations."

### "How long did this take?"
> "One night in Cursor. The design spec was clear, and Next.js + Tailwind + Framer Motion made it fast."

Imagine an AI BDR (like Artisan's Ava) working autonomously. Ava Mirror shows:

1. **Live task feed** — what she's doing right now

3. **Signal transparency** — the data points that informed each choice

## Key Tips for All Outreach

1. **Lead with the demo** — visuals beat words
2. **Keep it short** — respect their time
3. **Show, don't tell** — link to live demo or video
4. **Explain "why this matters"** — connect to their pain points
5. **Make it easy to respond** — ask one clear question
6. **Follow up once** — but don't spam
